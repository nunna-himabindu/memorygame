🧙‍♂️ Flip & Match — Magic Duel

A Harry Potter–themed two-player memory game where players flip cards, match magical emojis, and score points. Each player chooses a wizard character and gets a Hogwarts house assignment, with visual effects and sound enhancing the experience.

🎯 Game Highlights

Hogwarts Theme: Wizard names, characters, and house-based colors.

Interactive Board: Flip cards, match emojis, and lock matched cards with house colors.

Visual Effects: Background slideshow, floating magic particles, wand sparkles, and smooth card flip animations.

Sound Effects: Flip, match, and win sounds for immersive gameplay.

Responsive & Accessible: Works on desktop, tablet, mobile; supports keyboard navigation and ARIA labels.

🧩 How to Play

Enter Player 1 & 2 names and select characters.

Click “Enter the Magic World”.

Take turns flipping two cards.

Match: earn a point and keep your turn.

Mismatch: turn passes to the other player.

Game ends when all pairs are matched.

Winner: highest score; tie: draw.
